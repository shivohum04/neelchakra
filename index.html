Our Process

From Wire to Weather-Proof: Our 4-Stage Process

Every NeelChakra wire undergoes a rigorous 4-stage manufacturing process combining premium GI wire, specialized chemistry, and batch-level quality testing.

1

GI Foundation

Premium grade GI wire selection for first layer of corrosion defence — a solid, proven base for every NeelChakra product.

2

Chemical Dip

GI wire passes through a chemical dip for a second layer of protection from corrosion — relentless against rust.

3

Hot-Plate Adhesion

Chemical dip wire passes through a hot plate for proper adhesion of the chemical coating — ensuring deep, lasting bond at every point of the wire.

4

Salt-Spray Quality Check

Every NeelChakra wire batch undergoes salt-spray testing for quality check — zero compromise, every batch, every time.
